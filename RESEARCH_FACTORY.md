# Automated TRACE research workflow

A TRACE-specific implementation of the multi-agent *Paper Factory* idea lives in [\`research_factory/\`](research_factory/).

It is configured for DeepSeek through OpenRouter and tailored to the League of Nations bilingual transfer benchmark: retrieval ablations, agentic-vs-non-agentic comparisons, bilingual analysis, efficiency/cost, error analysis, robustness, an optional JEV-controller extension, three hard scientific gates, and final human review.

Start with [\`research_factory/README.md\`](research_factory/README.md).
